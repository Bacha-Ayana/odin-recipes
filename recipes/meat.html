Description.

This classic beef lasagna is a hearty, comforting dish that's perfect for family dinners or special occasions. Layers of tender pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta filling, and melted mozzarella cheese come together to create an irresistible Italian favorite.

The recipe uses a combination of ground beef and Italian sausage for maximum flavor, slow-simmered in a tomato-based sauce with herbs and spices. Baking brings all the components together into a golden, bubbly masterpiece that will have everyone coming back for seconds.
